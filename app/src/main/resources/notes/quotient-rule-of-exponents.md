# Quotient Rule of Exponents

The Quotient Rule of Exponents formula is used to divide exponential expressions
that have the same base.  
When you divide powers with the same base, you subtract the exponents.

$$
\frac{n^a}{n^b} = n^{a-b}
$$

### Example

The two $n$'s in the denominator cancel with two in the numerator:

$$
\frac{n^5}{n^2} = \frac{n \cdot n \cdot n \cdot n \cdot n}{n \cdot n} = n^3
$$
